Privacy Policy

LTW Live Class does not collect any data about you.

What stays on your device

The classes you add — names, meeting links and schedules — are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

No accounts, ads or tracking

There is no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party SDKs. The app does not track you across apps or websites.

Emails you share

When you share a Zoom email to the app, it is read on your device to find the meeting link and class dates. Only the link, a class name and the dates are kept; the email itself is not stored or sent anywhere.

Notifications

Class reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No push service is used.

Joining a class

When you tap GO, the app hands your meeting link to the Zoom app or your browser. From then on Zoom's own privacy policy applies to the meeting.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: github.com/karman195/ltw-live-class/issues.

Effective November 5, 2026.
