LTW Live Class

Join your Zoom class with one tap, mic and camera already off.

Download

Windows: Download for Windows (Windows 10 or 11). If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”, choose More info → Run anyway.

iPhone, iPad and Mac: on the App Store as LTW Live Class.

Add a class

  • Share the email. Open the Zoom registration email in Mail or Gmail, tap Share, choose LTW Live Class. The link and every class date are filled in.
  • Paste it. Copy the email or just the link, open the app and tap Paste.
  • Calendar file. Open the email's .ics attachment with LTW Live Class, or drop it on the Mac window.

Mic and camera off

Tap Set up mic & camera once. It walks you through Zoom's own “Always mute my microphone” and “Always turn off my video” settings, so every class you join from the app starts muted with the camera off.

Reminders

Choose 5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes before each class. Tap the notification to join straight away.

Questions or problems

Open an issue at github.com/karman195/ltw-live-class/issues and we'll get back to you.
